Gom: invite, sign-in, friends, group members

Names, IDs and the QR code are samples. The password field is a placeholder: how people sign in (password, passkey, other) is not decided yet.

1. Invite and sign-in

Sign-up from an invite link (it also joins a group), sign-in with a wrong-password state, and a dead invite link. On desktop these are the same centred column.

2. Starting a conversation and adding friends

The pencil button opens the picker. "Thêm bạn" opens the add-friend screen: exact Gom ID, your own QR and link, incoming and sent requests.

3. Group members

Members you are not yet friends with show "Thêm bạn". "Thêm thành viên" offers only your own friends.

4. Desktop: add friend as a dialog over the chat
